Information you enter

The enquiry form asks for your name, an optional phone number, an enquiry type, an optional product reference and your message. This website has no server: it does not send, receive or store these details. They are placed into a WhatsApp message that you choose to send.

Local storage

Your cart (product, option and quantity only) is stored in your browser using localStorage so it persists between visits. It stays on your device and you can clear it from the cart page or your browser settings. No payment details are collected.

WhatsApp

When you continue to WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. Messages you send are then received by the store.
